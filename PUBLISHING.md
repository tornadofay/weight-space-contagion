## Before a formal tagged release or archival DOI
- Verify citations and bibliographic metadata against primary sources.
- Complete the systematic literature review or clearly retain its preliminary status.
- Review equations, links, metadata, spelling, and navigation.
- Decide the repository content license explicitly; none is currently selected.
- Confirm that no restricted model weights, datasets, personal data, secrets, or unsafe operational material are included.
- Update `CITATION.cff` to the exact released version and date.

Do not represent a GitHub-hosted working paper as an arXiv submission, journal article, peer-reviewed paper, or DOI-archived publication. Keep the GitHub repository as the version-controlled source.
