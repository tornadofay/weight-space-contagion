# Publication and Collaboration Plan

## Current public artifact

The current manuscript is a conceptual problem statement and threat-model proposal. It describes donor-guided conditional-behavior implantation, separates a one-generation P2 question from the stronger P3 generational question, and maps relevant prior work. It contains no original experimental results and is not peer reviewed.

## Scope and author commitment

The repository author is not conducting experiments, does not claim the necessary research team or infrastructure, and makes no commitment to produce experimental results. The repository is not an implementation project or an experimental benchmark. The evaluation considerations are intended to make the question clearer for specialists and independent teams that may have the appropriate resources.

## Responsible next steps

1. **Improve the problem statement:** incorporate corrections, relevant sources, and feedback from researchers in model editing, neural-network security, artificial life, and AI safety.
2. **Resolve prior-art questions:** continue the preliminary literature review, especially the full-text comparison of Randazzo et al. (2021), and avoid priority claims while relevant overlap remains unresolved.
3. **Invite independent expert assessment:** ask qualified researchers whether the distinctions are technically meaningful, whether the donor-guided scenario is already covered by existing work, and what terminology or evidence thresholds should be corrected.
4. **Independent research, if adopted by others:** any team choosing to investigate the problem would own its scientific protocol, approvals, compute, model access, experiments, analysis, and resulting claims. Such work is not promised or represented as being conducted by this repository's author.
5. **Archival release:** consider a DOI-backed version after expert feedback, literature corrections, metadata, and license decisions have been addressed.
6. **Venue submission:** consider a suitable position-paper, security, ML, artificial-life, or AI-safety venue only if the contribution and venue expectations are appropriate.

## Claims policy

- Do not call the proposed mechanism demonstrated, discovered, autonomous, or a working AI virus.
- Do not claim novelty or priority from the preliminary literature review.
- A conceptual problem statement is not an empirical paper or a peer-reviewed result.
- Separate model-file replication, one-generation behavior transfer, generational parameter-space transmission, and autonomy.
- Do not imply that the repository author has run or will run tests or experiments.
- Do not fabricate affiliations, ORCID, funding, ethics approval, results, or peer-review status.
