# Publication Plan

## Current public artifact
The position paper is a conceptual research agenda. It defines a falsifiable question, maps adjacent work, formalizes claim levels, and proposes a safe staged research programme. It contains no original experimental results and is not peer reviewed.

## Publication sequence
1. **Public working paper (current):** publish the conceptual paper, definitions, preliminary related-work map, bibliography, and research constitution in the public GitHub repository.
2. **Systematic literature review:** document databases, exact search strings, screening, full-text comparisons, and a revised novelty assessment. Pay particular attention to Gabor et al. (2022), model-merging backdoor transfer, and AI weight-based replicators.
3. **Preregistered pilot:** define a harmless synthetic marker, fixed controls, evaluation splits, thresholds, budget, and stop conditions before running the experiment.
4. **Empirical report:** publish all outcomes, including null and failed runs; disclose the editor, tools, model identities, compute, deviations, and limitations.
5. **Archival release:** after review, consider Zenodo or another repository that assigns a DOI. Keep GitHub as the version-controlled source.
6. **Venue submission:** select a suitable security, ML, artificial-life, or AI-safety venue only after the contribution is supported by the literature review and evidence.

## Claims policy
- Do not call the proposed mechanism demonstrated, discovered, autonomous, or a working AI virus.
- Do not claim novelty or priority from the preliminary web search.
- A conceptual position paper is a legitimate public research artifact, but it is not equivalent to a peer-reviewed empirical paper.
- Separate model-file replication, one-generation backdoor inheritance, second-generation parameter-space reproduction, and autonomy.
- Do not fabricate affiliations, ORCID, funding, ethics approval, results, or peer-review status.
