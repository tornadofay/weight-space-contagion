# Research Status

**As of:** 2026-11-04  
**Repository:** https://github.com/tornadofay/weight-space-contagion  
**Current stage:** Public conceptual working paper and preliminary literature review.

## Established
- Central question and operational terminology defined.
- Claim levels distinguish host-level replication, behavioral persistence, parameter-space transmission, generational reproduction, and autonomy.
- Competing explanations and minimum evidence thresholds documented.
- Offline, owner-controlled, benign-marker safety boundary recorded.
- Preliminary literature map includes Gabor et al. (2022), Pan et al. (2025), Air et al. (2026), Anthropic (2026), Yuan et al. (2025), Cui et al. (2026), Ainsworth et al. (2023), and Takehi et al. (2026).
- A conceptual position paper and systematic-review protocol are prepared.

## Not established
- No experiment has been run by this project.
- No parameter-space transmission has been demonstrated.
- No generational reproduction has been demonstrated.
- No autonomy claim is made.
- Novelty has not been established; the review is preliminary and non-systematic.
- No peer-reviewed submission, arXiv submission, DOI, or external publication has occurred.

## Next gates
1. Execute the systematic literature review, including full-text comparison with Gabor et al. (2022) and Merge Hijacking (2025).
2. Revise the novelty statement based on screening results.
3. Preregister the harmless synthetic-marker protocol and controls before any holdout evaluation.
4. Conduct only offline, owner-controlled experiments with bounded, reversible interventions.
5. Update the paper only from recorded evidence; retain negative and inconclusive outcomes.

## Publication status
The GitHub repository is public. The manuscript is a public working paper hosted in the repository; it is not peer reviewed and is not an arXiv or journal publication. Consider a formal release and archival DOI only after reviewing repository contents, metadata, citations, and license decisions.
