# Research Status

**As of:** 2026-11-04  
**Repository:** https://github.com/tornadofay/weight-space-contagion  
**Current stage:** Public conceptual problem statement, threat-model proposal, and preliminary literature synthesis.

## Established
- The donor-guided conditional-behavior implantation scenario is documented as a hypothetical one-generation P2 problem.
- P2 one-generation transfer is distinguished from P3 generational parameter-space transmission and P4 autonomy.
- The paper separates capability transfer, policy/refusal changes, task routing, and model-layer versus harness-layer effects.
- The conceptual question, provisional operational terminology, and evidence boundaries are documented.
- The paper and related-work map now acknowledge foundational neural self-replication research from 2018 onward, including Neural Network Quine, Gabor et al. (2019, 2021, 2022), Randazzo et al. (2021), collaborative organism networks (2022), and Sinapayen's NCA pattern replication with inheritable mutation (2023).
- Competing explanations and minimum evidence thresholds are documented as questions for independent research teams.
- Offline, owner-controlled, benign-marker safety boundaries are recorded for any future empirical study.
- A conceptual working paper, preliminary bibliography, literature-review protocol, and evaluation considerations are available.

## Not established
- No experiment or test has been run by this project.
- The repository author is not conducting or committing to experiments and does not claim access to the infrastructure or team required to do so.
- No implementation, benchmark, or empirical validation is provided.
- No parameter-space transmission has been demonstrated by this project.
- No multi-generation transmission has been demonstrated by this project.
- No autonomy claim is made.
- Novelty has not been established; the review is preliminary and non-systematic.
- Sinapayen (2023) demonstrates multi-generation replication and inheritable mutations in NCA patterns/cell states under fixed learned update rules; it is adjacent but is not evidence of transferring the NCA rule-network weights into another model.
- Randazzo et al. (2021), *Recursively Fertile Self-replicating Neural Agents*, is a particularly close predecessor. The lead author's public note describes feedforward networks that encode an image and self-replicate with and without variation, and a genealogy of neural networks. The full proceedings paper was not available in the sources inspected; its exact overlap with P3 remains unresolved and blocks any novelty claim.
- The new literature additions include answer-side and positional backdoor research, cross-model trigger-transfer limitations, parameter-editing collateral effects, and model-layer versus harness-layer security. Their relevance is contextual; none is treated as evidence of the complete proposed mechanism.
- No journal or conference submission, arXiv submission, DOI archival release, or peer-reviewed acceptance has occurred.
